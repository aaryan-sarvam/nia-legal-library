# Acts

20 central Acts, one PDF each. Amendment Acts are in [amendments/](../amendments/); rules, orders and the
UAPA schedules are in [rules-orders-schedules/](../rules-orders-schedules/).

## Which law applies

Three new codes replaced the old ones on 1 July 2024. The old codes still govern older matters, so both are here.

| New code, in force from 1 July 2024 | Replaced | The old code still governs |
|---|---|---|
| Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS): procedure | Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC) | investigations, trials and applications pending on 1 July 2024 (BNSS s. 531) |
| Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS): offences | Indian Penal Code, 1860 (IPC) | offences committed before 1 July 2024 (BNS s. 358) |
| Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA): evidence | Indian Evidence Act, 1872 | proceedings begun before 1 July 2024 (BSA s. 170) |
